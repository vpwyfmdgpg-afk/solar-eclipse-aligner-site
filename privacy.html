Privacy Policy

Solar Eclipse Aligner

Last updated: 15 August 2026

Overview

Solar Eclipse Aligner is designed to process solar eclipse photographs locally on the user's device.

Data Collection

Solar Eclipse Aligner does not require a user account and does not collect personal information from users.

The app does not use advertising, tracking, or analytics services.

Photographs and Files

Images and folders selected by the user are accessed only for the purpose of analysing, aligning, previewing, and exporting eclipse image sequences.

User photographs are processed locally on the device and are not uploaded to external servers by Solar Eclipse Aligner.

Exported Files

Exported images and videos are saved only to locations selected by the user.

Third-Party Services

Solar Eclipse Aligner does not share user photographs or personal data with advertisers, data brokers, or third-party analytics providers.

Children's Privacy

Solar Eclipse Aligner does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will always be available on this page.
